Privacy Policy

Last updated 8 November 2026

10,000 Hours (“the app”, “we”) helps you track deliberate practice. This policy explains what the app collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. In short: we collect only what the app needs to work, we never sell your data, and you can delete it at any time.

What we collect

DataWhenWhy
Your skills, practice sessions, session notes, skill-tree steps and settingsAlways, stored on your phoneTo run the app
A copy of the above in our databaseOnly if you sign inBackup and syncing between your devices
Your Apple account identifier and, if you choose to share it, your name and emailWhen you use Sign in with AppleTo identify your account
What you type into the AI coach (your learning goal and your answers to its questions) and the names of skill steps you open lessons forOnly when you use the coachTo create plans and lessons
How many coach plans and lessons you have created this monthWhen you use the coachTo apply fair-use limits
Reports you send about a lessonOnly if you report oneTo review and fix lessons

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, health data, advertising identifiers or browsing history. The app has no ads and no third-party analytics or tracking.

Who we share it with

We use a small number of service providers, who process data only to provide their service to us:

  • Supabase hosts our database and sign-in, for signed-in users.
  • Anthropic provides the Claude AI model behind the coach. When you use the coach, your goal, your answers and the step names you request lessons for are sent to Anthropic to generate a response. We do not send your name, email or practice history. The app asks for your permission before your first use of the coach.
  • Apple provides Sign in with Apple, push notifications and app distribution.
  • Expo delivers app updates. It receives basic technical information such as app version and device type.

Lessons are stored by topic (for example “Swimming: breathing”) and may be shown to other users learning the same thing. They never contain your personal details.

We do not sell or rent your personal data, and we do not use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

Data on your phone stays until you delete it or remove the app. Data in your account stays until you delete your account. Coach usage counts are kept for the current month and the previous one.

Your choices and rights

  • Use it without an account. Everything except syncing and the AI coach works without signing in.
  • Delete your account in the app: Profile → Delete account. This permanently removes your account and everything stored with it.
  • Erase data on your phone: Settings → Erase everything.
  • Export a copy of your data: Settings → Export a backup.
  • Depending on where you live (for example under the UK GDPR, EU GDPR or California law) you may have rights to access, correct, delete or move your data, and to object to its use. Contact us to use them.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Each account can only read its own data, enforced by the database itself. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above, and tell you in the app if the change is significant.
